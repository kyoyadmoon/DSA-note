# Mission: Data Structures and Algorithms Interview Readiness

## Why

Build the ability to solve data-structure and algorithm interview problems under time pressure, while explaining why the chosen structure is correct and how its time and space costs are derived.

## Success looks like

- Recognize the data structure or algorithmic pattern implied by a new problem.
- State the invariant, choose operations deliberately, and derive their complexity without memorizing a table in isolation.
- Implement the core structures and algorithms in TypeScript without relying on library shortcuts.
- Explain trade-offs, edge cases, and follow-up optimizations while coding.
- Transfer each chapter into practice on representative interview problems.

## Constraints

- Chapters use Traditional Chinese explanations with TypeScript implementations.
- Every chapter includes an interactive trace, tests, retrieval prompts, complexity analysis, and representative problems.
- Changes are split into one linear pull request per chapter so each topic can be reviewed independently.
- No interview date or target company has been provided, so the curriculum prioritizes generally common software-engineering interviews.

## Out of scope

- System design, operating systems, networking, and behavioral interview preparation.
- Specialized structures with low general interview frequency until the core curriculum is complete.
